KB-310 AC Leaking Water or Not Cooling

Service knowledge base

  1. Common causes: clogged condensate line, frozen coil, failed condensate pump.
  2. Advise caller to place a towel or pan and keep water away from outlets. If water reaches electrical equipment, treat as electrical hazard.
  3. Required skill: ac. Priority P3 unless the system is down in extreme heat.
