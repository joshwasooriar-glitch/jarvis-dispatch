KB-110 Furnace Not Heating

Service knowledge base

  1. Common causes: failed ignitor, dirty flame sensor, tripped limit switch, thermostat settings, clogged filter.
  2. Escalate to URGENT (P1) when indoor temperature is 60F or below, or a vulnerable occupant is present (elderly, infant, medical needs).
  3. Required skill: furnace. Bring ignitor and flame sensor stock.
  4. Dispatcher must not walk caller through opening the furnace cabinet.
