SOP-02 Carbon Monoxide Alarm

Life safety. Dispatcher procedure

  1. Treat any CO alarm or headache, nausea, or dizziness near fuel-burning equipment as an emergency.
  2. Instruct everyone, including pets, to get to fresh air and call 911 from outside.
  3. Page the on-call human dispatcher and assign an on-call gas-certified technician.
  4. Priority P0. Do not schedule a routine visit.

Demo content for JARVIS Dispatch. Not real safety guidance for any company.
