SOP-01 Gas Odor Emergency

Life safety. Dispatcher procedure

  1. Never diagnose a gas odor over the phone. Read the approved script verbatim.
  2. Instruct the caller to evacuate everyone immediately and not operate switches, appliances, or open flames.
  3. Caller must phone 911 or the gas utility emergency line from outside the home.
  4. Page the on-call human dispatcher within 2 minutes and assign the nearest on-call gas-certified technician.
  5. Technician may not enter until the utility or fire department clears the home.
  6. Required: gas_certified credential. Priority P0, response target 90 minutes.
